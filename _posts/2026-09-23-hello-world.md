Hi, I’m Nathaniel Bubis. Welcome to my blog.

I like projects that turn interesting ideas into working software. Geometry and computer vision are two recurring interests: my public projects include [Poincare](https://github.com/nbubis/Poincare), a Python library for hyperbolic geometry constructions, and [AutoStitching](https://github.com/nbubis/AutoStitching), an image stitching project.

I’m starting this site to write down what I build, the questions I run into, and what I learn while solving them. Expect technical notes, project updates, and the occasional explanation of an idea that deserves a closer look.

If you’d like to follow along, this blog has an [RSS feed](/feed.xml). You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/nbubis).
